# What the evidence says (one page, facts and limits only)

All numbers are R per trade (R = the amount at risk, the distance from entry to the initial stop), after 5 bps taker fees per side, 11 bps round-trip slippage and historical funding, four pairs (BTC, ETH, BNB, XRP), 1h candles unless stated, from 2020 to 2026. Costs are assumptions of the studies, not measurements of your account. Every figure is a measurement on past data. None is a forecast.

**The three strategies as they run today (1h).** Donchian: -0.17 R per trade (5,296 trades, 2022 to 2026), negative in every one of 9 six-month windows, distinguishable from zero. RSI: -0.29 R (624 trades), negative in all 9 windows. EMA: -0.12 R (2,659 trades), positive in 4 of 9 windows, not distinguishable from zero; EMA was +0.06 R per trade over 2020 to 2024 and -0.30 in 2025.

**Costs or signals.** Costs take 0.14 to 0.20 R per trade (fees about 0.06 to 0.09, slippage 0.07 to 0.10, funding up to 0.02). Donchian's signal earns about 0.00 R before costs, so its loss is the cost. RSI's signal loses about 0.09 to 0.11 R before costs (about a third of its loss is the signal). EMA's signal earned +0.2 R before costs in 2020 to 2024 and about +0.03 R over 2022 to 2026.

**Possible changes.**
- *Fee tier, BNB, maker orders:* paying fees in BNB adds about +0.006 to +0.009 R per trade; maker entries add up to +0.05 to +0.08 R if every order fills (missed fills can erase it); a VIP tier needs volume far above a pilot's (fee schedule UNVERIFIED). With the best case none of Donchian (-0.06 to -0.08) or RSI (-0.18) turns positive; with zero cost Donchian is about 0.00 R and RSI -0.09 to -0.11 R. Slippage on stops and funding are not the owner's to change.
- *Exit rules (+1R lock, take profit, trail):* no consistent net gain for EMA, a loss for Donchian, none for RSI (its target is hit first). The lock cuts the share of trades that give back a +1R profit (EMA 50-64% to 2-6%).
- *Entry filters, guards, pair choice, walk-forward choice:* none of 31 candidates passed; the best was within noise of doing nothing.
- *Higher timeframes (4h, 1d):* the cost per trade falls to 0.05 to 0.12 R (stops of 3% to 10%). EMA on 4h and 1d is +0.24 to +0.72 R per trade over 2022 to 2025, but not distinguishable from zero (a few windows carry the gain; 72 to 793 trades); Donchian 4h/1d +0.01 to +0.11; RSI 4h 0.00. Nothing passed the pre-registered rule; the later windows were not opened.

**The 100-trade pilot.** One trade's R varies a lot (standard deviation 0.8 R for RSI, 1.4 for Donchian, 2.8 for EMA on 1h; 4.8 for EMA on 4h). A 100-trade average therefore has a 95% interval of about plus or minus 0.16 R (RSI), 0.27 (Donchian), 0.55 (EMA 1h) and 0.94 (EMA 4h) around the true mean. Resampling the real trades 20,000 times: a 100-trade pilot ends with a positive total in about 0% of cases for RSI, 10% for Donchian, 29% for EMA 1h (and 80% for EMA 4h in-sample, whose edge is not established), and ends below -10 R in 99%, 72% and 59% of cases for RSI, Donchian and EMA 1h. So 100 trades can show a clearly negative result for RSI and Donchian if the history holds, and cannot separate EMA from zero. Time to 100 trades on ONE pair: Donchian 1h about 4 months, EMA 1h 8 months, RSI 1h about 34 months, EMA 4h about 33 months, EMA 1d about 18 years.

**Limits.** Costs, the fee schedule, maker fills and the archive data (public Binance files, not the live API) are assumptions or UNVERIFIED. 31 entry candidates and 9 timeframe cells were tested; a few passes by chance would have been expected, none occurred. Past results in a different regime (2025 was negative for EMA) need not repeat. The 4h and 1d numbers use 2020 to 2025 only and come from far fewer trades.
